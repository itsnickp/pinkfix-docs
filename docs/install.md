# Install

Pink Fix is a Blender extension for **Blender 4.2 LTS to 5.2 LTS**, tested in CI on Blender
4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 on Windows, macOS and Linux. It has no other requirements and never connects to the internet.

## From Superhive (recommended)

1. Buy Pink Fix on Superhive and open the **receipt page**.
2. Drag the extension from the receipt page into an open Blender window.
3. Blender asks whether to install it. Accept, and accept the offer to add **Superhive** as
   a remote repository: that is how you get updates later (see
   [Updates from Superhive](updates.md)).

## From the downloaded zip

1. Download `pink_fix-<version>.zip` from your Superhive account. **Don't unzip it.**
2. In Blender open **Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions**.
3. Open the menu at the top right of that tab (the **⌄** button) and choose
   **Install from Disk…**, then pick the zip.
4. Pink Fix appears under **Edit > Preferences > Add-ons**, enabled. Its version is shown
   when you expand the entry.

You can also drag the zip file straight onto the Blender window.

## Find it

In the 3D Viewport press ++n++ to open the sidebar and click the **Pink Fix** tab.
Scan, Match, Apply, Collect & Pack and Export Report are also in **File > External Data**. Then follow the [Quick start](index.md).

## Settings

**Edit > Preferences > Add-ons > Pink Fix** holds the defaults: search folders, Auto-Accept, Fuzzy Minimum, which file extensions to search and the default
Collect folder. See [Panels](panels.md#preferences).

## Update or remove

- **Update:** through the Superhive repository, see [Updates from Superhive](updates.md).
  Installing a newer zip from disk also replaces the old version.
- **Remove:** **Edit > Preferences > Add-ons**, expand **Pink Fix**, then **Uninstall**
  (in the entry's **⌄** menu).
